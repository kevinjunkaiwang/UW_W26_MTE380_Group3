Detailed Design – Propeller and Motor Sizing
Section 1: Objective
The objective of this calculation is to determine the size of the two
motors that drive the propellers. The desired speed can be used to
determine the required thrust, based on the propeller size, a motor can
be selected.
The size of the motor includes the revolutions per minute (RPM) and
the power in Watts. An important parameter of the motor is the motor
constant (RPM/volt), which determines the RPM given an applied
voltage.

Section 4: Assumptions
The following assumptions have been made in this calculation:
1. The drag from air has been neglected since it is expected to be
negligible compared to the drag of the water.
2. The initial calculation will be done at steady state when the
acceleration is zero.
3. A propeller is selected. This may be iterative.

Section 7: Results / Conclusions
The required motor must be able to reach a speed of 2400 rpm and
produce 6.7 W of power. A motor that will provide the RPM and power
is the Turnigy AquaStar 4084-620 Kv brushless motor from
hobbyking.com.
Based on available motors, it is suggested that the design specifications
are revisited and a higher boat velocity considered.
